New patients

Your first visit, step by step.

Here’s what to expect, what to bring, and how insurance and payment work.

Request appointment (856) 553-8724

What to expect

From first call to first visit

  1. Request a time

    Give us a call or send a request online, and one of our team will get in touch.

  2. Complete your forms

    You’ll be asked to fill out some forms and provide information about yourself and your reason for seeking help.

  3. Initial consultation

    45–60 minutes, in person in Vineland or by video, with a qualified mental health professional.

  4. Start your plan

    Individual, family or group therapy, medication, or other interventions, with follow-up care.

What to bring

Your first-visit checklist

  • Your insurance card
  • Your medication list
  • Medical history or a referral letter, if you have one
  • Your questions and concerns, written down
  • Childcare arrangements, if needed
  • Arrive early for paperwork and check-in

Forms & client portal

Existing patients can use our client portal (TherapyPortal) for forms and requests. After your first appointment is scheduled, we’ll send you an invitation to set up your client portal account.

FAQ

New patient questions

Can’t find your answer? Call (856) 553-8724.

Who can benefit from care at Oakland?

Anyone who is experiencing distress, impairment, or dissatisfaction in their personal, social, or occupational functioning may benefit. Common reasons people seek help include depression, anxiety, stress, trauma, addiction, bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, personality disorders, eating disorders and mood disorders. You do not need a specific diagnosis or problem to visit. If you feel you need support, guidance or treatment, you are welcome to contact us.

Do you see children and teens?

Yes. We diagnose and treat individuals across the lifespan, including children ages 4–17, adults and the elderly.

What happens at the first visit?

You will be greeted by friendly, compassionate staff who respect your privacy and confidentiality. You’ll fill out some forms, then meet with a qualified mental health professional who will conduct an assessment and discuss your goals and treatment options with you. Initial consultations take 45–60 minutes. Depending on your needs, you may receive individual, family or group therapy, medication, or other interventions, with follow-up care as needed.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. You can schedule an in-person visit at our Vineland office or a video session with one of our providers.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept all major insurance providers, including Medicaid, Medicare, Horizon, UnitedHealthcare, Cigna, Amerigroup and AmeriChoice. We also accept self-pay. For questions about fees and payment options, call us at (856) 553-8724.

Can I get an appointment in the evening or on the weekend?

Yes. We’re open Monday to Friday from 8 AM to 8 PM, and weekend appointments are available.

What if it’s an emergency?

Please don’t use this website or our forms in an emergency. For a mental health emergency, call 911. For the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, call or text 988. For the Crisis Text Line, text HOME to 741741. All are free, confidential and available 24/7.

How should I prepare for my first visit?

Write down any questions or concerns you have. Gather relevant documents, such as your medical history, medication list, insurance card or referral letter. Arrange childcare if needed, and arrive early to complete paperwork and check-in. Be honest and open about your needs, and be patient with yourself and the process of recovery.

How much will it cost?

The cost may vary depending on the type and duration of services you receive and your insurance coverage. Contact us directly and ask our representatives about fees and payment options.

Can you refer me to other specialists?

Yes. We may also provide referrals to other resources or specialists as needed.
