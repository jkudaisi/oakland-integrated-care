How it works

Getting started is simple.

We are a dial away. Give us a call or send a request, and our experts will take it from there.

  1. Reach out

    Request an appointment online or call (856) 553-8724. In-person and video visits available.

  2. Initial consultation

    Meet a qualified mental health professional for 45–60 minutes to discuss your goals and treatment options.

  3. Your treatment plan

    Our providers work with you to find the best care for all your mental health needs.

  4. Ongoing support

    Treatment management and follow-ups, plus counseling to help sustain the new, healthy you.

More for new patients
Who can benefit from care at Oakland?

Anyone who is experiencing distress, impairment, or dissatisfaction in their personal, social, or occupational functioning may benefit. Common reasons people seek help include depression, anxiety, stress, trauma, addiction, bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, personality disorders, eating disorders and mood disorders. You do not need a specific diagnosis or problem to visit. If you feel you need support, guidance or treatment, you are welcome to contact us.

Do you see children and teens?

Yes. We diagnose and treat individuals across the lifespan, including children ages 4–17, adults and the elderly.

What happens at the first visit?

You will be greeted by friendly, compassionate staff who respect your privacy and confidentiality. You’ll fill out some forms, then meet with a qualified mental health professional who will conduct an assessment and discuss your goals and treatment options with you. Initial consultations take 45–60 minutes. Depending on your needs, you may receive individual, family or group therapy, medication, or other interventions, with follow-up care as needed.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. You can schedule an in-person visit at our Vineland office or a video session with one of our providers.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept all major insurance providers, including Medicaid, Medicare, Horizon, UnitedHealthcare, Cigna, Amerigroup and AmeriChoice. We also accept self-pay. For questions about fees and payment options, call us at (856) 553-8724.

Can I get an appointment in the evening or on the weekend?

Yes. We’re open Monday to Friday from 8 AM to 8 PM, and weekend appointments are available.

Need help right now?

Please don’t use this website or our forms in an emergency. If you or someone you love is in danger, call 911. For a mental health crisis, call or text 988 any time, or text HOME to 741741.
