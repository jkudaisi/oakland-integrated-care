Is it time to reach out?

Signs it may be time to talk to someone

Depression

  • Feeling sad excessively, nearly daily, in the last 2 weeks
  • Losing interest in things you once loved to do
  • Difficulty sleeping or excessive fatigue
  • Excessive guilt or worthlessness, or thoughts of suicide or death

Anxiety

  • Trouble sleeping nearly daily in the last 6 months
  • Restlessness
  • Increased irritability
  • Trouble concentrating

ADHD

  • Struggling to give attention to details
  • Hyperactivity or talkativeness
  • Restlessness and impulsivity
  • Impact on daily life and relationships

Bipolar disorder

  • More talkative, irritable or easily distractible
  • Decreased need for sleep
  • Fluctuating mood, restlessness or depression
  • Suicidal thoughts in the last week

Autism (for parents)

  • Difficulty with nonverbal communicative behaviors
  • Resistance to change in routines, or rituals
  • Difficulty with social-emotional reciprocity
  • Difficulty developing and maintaining relationships

Substance abuse

  • Excessive or inappropriate substance use
  • Difficulty cutting down or stopping
  • Withdrawal symptoms or cravings
  • A loved one who is struggling with substance use

These lists are not a diagnosis. Only a qualified clinician can evaluate your symptoms. If you are having thoughts of suicide, call or text 988 now.
