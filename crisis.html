Get help now

You are not alone. Help is available 24/7.

You can get free and confidential 24/7 support from the resources below. Please don’t use this website or our forms in an emergency.

Emergency: call 911

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, has been seriously hurt, or may act on thoughts of suicide right now, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
