Our commitment

This website is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

Site features

  • Large, high-contrast text (18px minimum body size) with generous line spacing
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus outlines and a “skip to main content” link
  • Descriptive labels and error messages on every form field
  • Motion is reduced automatically if your device asks for it
  • Pages work when zoomed to 200% and on small screens

At our office

Our office is wheelchair accessible. We’re in the first building on your right as you drive in. If you need any help getting to Suite 2A, let us know when you call and we’ll be ready for you. Spanish-speaking support is available.

Feedback

If anything on this site is hard to use, please tell us and we’ll help you get the information another way. Call (856) 553-8724 or email contact@oaklandcare.org.
