About Oakland

Quality care that treats the whole person.

At Oakland Integrated, we provide evidence-based, patient-centered, trauma-informed, quality care to improve mental health and treat individuals with mental illness.

What we do

Care across the lifespan

We diagnose and treat individuals across the lifespan, including children ages 4–17, adults and the elderly.

Our experienced providers specialize in addiction medicine, child and adolescent psychiatry, short-term and long-term care, and outpatient management.

  • Finding the right treatment. Our providers work with you to find the best care for all your mental health needs.
  • Treatment management & follow-ups. We don’t stop at finding the right treatment. We manage your care all through your treatment phase.
  • Doing things differently. Once you’re feeling better, we provide counseling on how to do things differently to sustain the new, healthy you.

Evidence-based

Psychotherapy and pharmacologic interventions to improve mental health.

Patient-centered

Comprehensive, holistic, individualized treatment options.

Trauma-informed

Quality care for individuals living with mental illness, including trauma-focused treatment.
